Why we’re here

Bridging generations through technology.

Technology can bring people closer—but it can also feel confusing or intimidating. We pair student volunteers with older adults for welcoming, practical learning that meets each person where they are.

♡

Patient, personal support

Every question is welcome. We go at the learner’s pace and practice together.

⌁

Useful everyday skills

Lessons focus on the technology people use to communicate, learn, and stay independent.

♧

Safer digital habits

We share practical ways to spot suspicious messages and protect private information.

What we teach

Technology made approachable.

Our workshops are designed to be hands-on, friendly, and useful from the very first session.

▣

Phone & computer basics

Settings, Wi-Fi, browsing, email, and getting comfortable with everyday devices.

☏

Stay in touch

Practice texting, sharing photos, and making video calls with family and friends.

♢

Online safety

Learn warning signs of scams, safer passwords, and when to ask a trusted person for help.
